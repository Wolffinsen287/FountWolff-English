Meet your teacher

English is more than a subject. It is access.

FWE's founder and head instructor, Daniel Wolff, began his career as a New York City legal proofreader, and has since turned his command of the English language into a way to help professionals reach goals they'd once thought out of reach.

With a solid background in pedagogy, Daniel's approach centers on individualized instruction — creative, sometimes unconventional, always built around the student in front of him.

"The right words can change the room."— Daniel Wolff
